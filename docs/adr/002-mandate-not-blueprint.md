# ADR 002 — Mandate ≠ Blueprint

**Status:** Accepted (implemented)

**Date:** 2026-09-27

## Context

Citizens state delivery demands. Public-office seekers may later document proposals. Mixing these models produces ranking pressure and endorsement optics.

## Decision

1. **Citizen Mandate** records and **Public Blueprint** records are separate classes and separate Notion databases.
2. Public Mandate surfaces never display candidate rankings, scores, or "match %".
3. Blueprint publication requires dual independent human review, inspectable source, and explicit Publish decision.
4. Payment or political interest never changes publication state.

## Consequences

- Product remains a civic demand instrument first.
- Blueprint pilot can grow without rewriting Mandate Status semantics.
- Intelligence layers must wait until field Published volume and governance capacity justify them.
