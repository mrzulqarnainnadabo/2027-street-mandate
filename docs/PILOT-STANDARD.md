# Civic Mandate Nigeria — Pilot standard (what we claim)

## We claim

- A **human-reviewed public record** of concrete public-service demands.
- Each **Published** row has duty, place (state/optional LGA), and a concrete demand sentence.
- **Civic Intelligence** and Pulse reflect **Published** rows only.
- Zero published is an **empty public record**, not a ranking and not system failure.

## We do not claim

- National public opinion or “what Nigerians want” as a population.
- Representative sampling across states or demographics.
- That submission causes government action.
- Candidate rankings, endorsements, or electability.
- Completeness of coverage outside the states present in Published rows.

## Language rules (product copy)

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|--------|
| Published records so far | What Nigerians are demanding (as census) |
| Pilot / growing public record | National survey results |
| States represented in the record | Nationwide mandate |
| Human-reviewed before publication | Instant public wall of all submissions |

## Data quality bar for publish

Operators should prefer publish when:

1. Demand is a concrete service/outcome (not slogan or pure attack).
2. Duty is the best available category.
3. State is correct; LGA spelling is consistent where provided.
4. No party campaign content or hate.

## Success metric for this pilot phase

- Clean Published rows that partners can cite with methodology limits.
- Submitters can check **status** and understand New → Published / Not published.
- Public surfaces never invent counts when the API is unavailable.
