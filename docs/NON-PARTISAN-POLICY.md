# Non-Partisan Policy — Civic Mandate Nigeria

ISEYC does not endorse candidates, parties, or policies through this platform.

## Forbidden product behaviours

- Rankings, match scores, electability predictions
- “Who should you vote for?”
- Pay-to-rank, pay-to-feature, paid endorsement, preferential visibility
- Campaign CRM / voter microtargeting

## Allowed

- Published citizen delivery demands (human-reviewed)
- Source-backed blueprints and profiles with equal process
- Verification states: Unverified | Source-linked | Institution-reviewed

**Institution-reviewed** means source integrity/completeness review only — not political support.
