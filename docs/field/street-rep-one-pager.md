# Street Rep one-pager — ISEYC 2027 Civic Mandate

**Print or screenshot. Use in ward / street conversations.**

## What this is

Citizens state **one concrete thing public office must deliver**.
ISEYC reviews. Only **Published** demands appear on the public wall and State Brief.

**Counts are not votes.** They are a public record of demands.

## What to say (30 seconds)

1. “This is not a poll and not about who to vote for.”
2. “Name a service outcome — for example medicine in the PHC, power for shops, water in the ward.”
3. “Choose your state, LGA if you know it, the duty, and the office that should deliver.”
4. “After ISEYC publishes it, it can appear on the State Brief we share in the group.”

## Quality gate (reject or rewrite before submit)

- Vague (“fix everything”) → ask for **one** measurable outcome
- Party / candidate slogan → rewrite as a **service** demand
- Several demands in one sentence → split into **one submit each**
- Wrong office → use the form hints / map (pilot taxonomy, not law)
