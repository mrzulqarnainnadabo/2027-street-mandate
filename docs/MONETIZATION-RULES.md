# Monetization Rules

## Forbidden

Payment must never change: publication priority, ranking, verification state, editorial treatment, public visibility, or source requirements.

No pay-to-rank, pay-to-feature, paid homepage placement, or paid “endorsed” badges.

## Allowed (future)

Institutional research subscriptions, CSO briefs, grants, equal-fee technical formatting (same SLA for all).

## V1

No candidate monetization UI in the current product increment.
