# ISEYC civic loop — Mandate + Brain

## One problem, two surfaces

**Problem:** Citizens and partners struggle to turn a concrete service demand into (1) an inspectable public record and (2) a careful recommendation of which institution may be responsible — without turning the tool into a poll, campaign, or auto-messaging machine.

```
Citizen demand
    → Civic Mandate (moderate → Publish)
        → public record (Pulse / Brief / Intelligence)
            → Civic Brain (explain published evidence)
            → Civic Brain (Mandate Routing recommendation only)
                → human operator review
                → (future, authorised only) verified referral + evidence
```

## Product split

| Product | Job | Does not |
|---------|-----|----------|
| **Civic Mandate Nigeria** | Capture, moderate, publish concrete demands by place and duty | Rank candidates, claim national opinion, auto-contact offices |
| **Civic Brain** | Explain published evidence; recommend institutions | Invent counts, send referrals, claim resolution |

## Partnership-facing value

Partners get:
1. A **methodology-bound** public record (Published only)
2. A **recommendation** path with human gates
3. Explicit limits: pilot coverage, no fake completeness

Partners do not get:
- Endorsement machinery
- Guaranteed government response
- Access to unpublished / private fields

## Pilot success metric

A Nigerian user can:
1. Submit a concrete demand and keep a status link
2. See only honest published counts (or unavailable — never fake zero-as-truth when load fails)
3. Ask what the public record shows without being told “Nigerians want X” as a survey claim
4. Get a routing recommendation that says **needs review** when jurisdiction is unclear
