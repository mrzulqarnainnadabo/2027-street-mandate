# Reviewer Recusal SOP (Draft)

**Product:** ISEYC 2027 Civic Mandate

## Purpose

Protect dual-review integrity when a reviewer has a conflict that could affect, or appear to affect, a Mandate or Blueprint decision.

This SOP does **not** require a multi-user staff account system. Operators may continue using shared process + named reviewer fields until a later identity system is approved.

## When to recuse

A reviewer **must not** act as Reviewer A or Reviewer B (or sole Mandate publisher) on a record if they have a **relevant**:

| Conflict type | Examples (non-exhaustive) |
|---------------|---------------------------|
| Political | Active role in a party campaign; declared intention to seek the same office the record concerns; formal endorsement of a named seeker on that record |
| Financial | Paid work for the submitter, party, or related PAC/consulting firm on that record |
| Personal | Close family of the named person on a Blueprint; material personal dispute with the submitter |
| Organisational | Decision-maker at an organisation that is the subject of the Mandate/response |

**When unsure:** recuse. Neutrality is architectural.

## What recusal means

1. Do **not** enter your name as Reviewer A or B on that record.  
2. Do **not** set Publication Decision / Mandate Status for that record.  
3. Hand the record to another qualified ISEYC reviewer.  
4. Optionally note in **internal** operator notes only: `Recused — [brief category]` — never publish recusal notes on public surfaces.

## What recusal does **not** mean

- Automatic rejection of the record  
- Public accusation  
- Blocking all review work by that person on unrelated records  
- Building a software permission system (deferred)

## Two-person rule (Blueprint)

Existing product rule remains:

- Reviewer A and Reviewer B must be **different people**  
- A person who recused cannot “clear” the record by acting as the other reviewer under another name

## Mandate publish

Where only one operator currently publishes Mandates:

- If that operator is conflicted, **another** ISEYC reviewer must complete Status → Published  
- If no second reviewer is available: leave Status = New and escalate to founder/ops lead

## Logging (minimum)

Until a formal audit log exists, keep a simple ops note (Notion comment or ops hub):

```
Date:
Record id:
Reviewer who recused:
Conflict category (political / financial / personal / organisational):
Who took over:
```

Do **not** put conflict details on public pages.
