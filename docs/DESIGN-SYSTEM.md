# ISEYC institutional design system — Civic Mandate Nigeria

This document applies the institution-wide identity to Civic Mandate Nigeria. The shared source of truth is the same canonical ISEYC identity and mark used by the Intelligence Observatory and Civic Brain. Do not introduce competing founder-pending taglines or substitute marks.

## Institutional identity

- Institution: Initiative for Sustainable Evolution for Youth and Community (ISEYC)
- Primary tagline: **Empowering Youth, Shaping Communities**
- Civic intelligence line: **Understanding Public Needs. Interpreting Evidence. Strengthening Accountability.**
- Product hierarchy: ISEYC is the institution; Civic Mandate Nigeria is the citizen entry point; the Intelligence Observatory is the evidence foundation; Civic Brain interprets published evidence; the Digital Operations Centre coordinates internal work.
- Departments: Education & Capacity Building; Health & Well-being; Software & Technology; Civic Department.
- Civic Mandate is a non-partisan learning and participation product. A submission is not a poll, endorsement, verified finding, or promise of government action.

## Official marks

Use the existing `public/iseyc-seal.svg` as the canonical compact institutional mark. Use the same supplied asset without redrawing, recolouring, cropping, distortion, replacement, or approximation. Do not create a substitute seal or generate new institutional artwork.

## Typography and responsive layout

Use an editorial serif for display headings and a readable sans-serif for interface text; use tabular numerals for data. Preserve local font fallbacks and low-bandwidth behavior. Keep the existing responsive navigation and single-column mobile layouts; maintain visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, accessible contrast, labelled statuses, and touch targets of at least 44px.

## Trust and privacy

Keep anonymous intake separate from the published public record. Only reviewed and approved records may appear on public surfaces; observations are not petitions and must remain private pending review. Preserve uncertainty and source limitations. Never describe a pilot record as representative public opinion or an outcome as independently verified without evidence.
